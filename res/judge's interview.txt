🎤 ÎNTREBAREA 2 (VERY COMMON)

“What was your biggest challenge and how did you overcome it?”

💡 RĂSPUNS MODEL

Our biggest challenge was balancing stability with speed.

Initially, improving stability made the robot heavier and slower.

We overcame this by redesigning the lift system using lighter materials and optimizing geometry instead of adding mass.

This allowed us to maintain speed while significantly improving stability.
