Our Story

Founded in 2023, the team Seramitae started as a small group of students driven by curiosity. Our goal was simple: to understand how things work and push beyond our limits.

Through failure, redesign, and persistence, we transform challenges into innovation. Today, we are a team of 15 members and 2 mentors, united by a vision: engineering solutions that create real impact.

We adhere to the STEM principles and we spread the FIRST values around us. We are grateful to all those who have contributed to this team over time and we work hard to enrich their legacy.

Robot

Please, meet our actual robot, named SERA.


Our robot uses a Mecanum wheel system for omnidirectional mobility, enbling precise movement in any direction. The intake mechanism is optimized for rapid collection of elements, while the linear elevator allow the precise placing of elements at various heights. We iterated the design three times for improving the stability and speed, achieving an increase of 40% in efficiency.
